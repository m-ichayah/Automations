# Basis and Scope

**Group C (29 Members) — Crude Distillation Unit Integrity Case Study**

## What this document set is

Four linked artefacts built on a single plant unit and a single causal chain:

| # | Artefact | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Root Cause Analysis (×3) | Why did these things fail? |
| 2 | Risk Register | What could still fail, and who is watching it? |
| 3 | Permit to Work System | Who authorises work on it, and under what control? |
| 4 | Job Safety Analysis | How is that work actually done without hurting anyone? |

Backward look → forward look → authorisation → execution.

## Status of the data

The unit, equipment tags, dates, dimensions, costs and inspection findings in
these documents are a **constructed teaching case**. They are built to be
internally consistent and technically representative of crude distillation
service, and every damage mechanism, code citation and control referenced is
real. They are **not** a record of any actual incident at any operating
facility, and none of the numbers should be quoted as operational data.

Where a figure is used in the presentation it is labelled *indicative*.

## The reference unit

Atmospheric Crude Distillation Unit (CDU), nominal 120,000 bpsd, processing a
medium sour blend. Assessment period: 12 months to 30 June 2026.

| Tag | Equipment | Service |
|---|---|---|
| T-1101 | Crude distillation tower | Atmospheric fractionation |
| H-1101 | Crude charge heater | Fired, 4-pass, 9Cr-1Mo radiant coil |
| E-1103A | Overhead condenser | Tower top vapour / cooling water |
| V-1103 | Overhead accumulator | Reflux and sour water separation |
| P-1104A/B | Heavy gas oil (HGO) pump | 290 °C hot hydrocarbon, API 610 OH2 |
| E-1108 | Crude preheat exchanger | Crude / cooling water |
| D-1101 | Desalter | Two-stage electrostatic |
